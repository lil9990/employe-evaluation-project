# Employee Evaluation System

## Overview
A web-based Employee Evaluation System developed as a COOP project during training at the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (Labor Office - Hail). The system allows customers to rate employees using a five-star rating system, enter their email address, and submit written feedback, while enabling managers to monitor all evaluations and employees to view and respond to their own feedback to improve service quality and transparency.

## Problem Statement and Benefits
- **Problem Addressed:** Traditional evaluation methods are often time-consuming, unclear, and lack direct customer feedback or employee visibility into feedback.
- **Key Benefits:**
  - Improves employee performance through continuous customer feedback.
  - Enables managers to monitor employee performance and manage employee data.
  - Enhances transparency between customers, employees, and management.
  - Allows employees to respond to customer feedback and identify strengths and areas for improvement.
  - Supports management decisions related to training and performance development.

## Key Features and Users
- **Customers:** Rate employees (1-5 stars), submit written feedback, and enter their email address without creating an account.
- **Employees:** Log in to view their personal evaluations and respond to customer feedback.
- **Managers / Admins:** Log in to view evaluations for all employees, respond to feedback, generate performance reports, and manage employee data.

## Technologies Used
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Node.js
- **Database:** SQLite
- **Security:** `bcryptjs` for password hashing and role-based access control

## System Design and Methodology
- Developed using a structured sequential approach covering requirement analysis, system design (Use Case, Class Diagram, ERD, Sequence Diagram), implementation, testing, and evaluation.
- **Scope Boundaries:** Focused strictly on employee evaluation and feedback functionalities via web browsers (excludes payroll management, recruitment, and task assignment).
